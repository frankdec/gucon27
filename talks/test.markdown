A (Grothendieck) topos \\(\mathcal{E}\\) is a lex reflexive subcategory of a presheaf category.
In this talk, I'll show that every proposition \\(\varphi : \Omega\\) in \\(\mathcal{E}\\) fractures \\(\mathcal{E}\\) into two complementary subtopoi.
Moreover, there is a geometric morphism \\(\mathcal{E} \rightarrow \mathcal{F}\\), where \\(\mathcal{F}\\) is the full subcategory of \\(\mathcal{E}\\) spanned by \\(\varphi\\)-modal objects.
